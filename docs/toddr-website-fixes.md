# toddr.ai website punch list

You own the edits, so these are written as do-this items. Ordered by how much each one hurts.
Based on the homepage copy as pasted on 2026-10-30; I have not been able to load the live site
from this environment, so check each item against what is actually there.

## Must do before any traffic is sent to the site

1. **Remove the Emma Wilson and Jen M. testimonials and both AI videos.** They are presented as
   customer testimonials and they are not. The FTC's 2024 rule on fake reviews and testimonials
   makes this a legal exposure, not just a taste issue (verify the rule at ftc.gov, but do not
   wait on that to remove them). Also remove the "+5,724% ... 23 to 1,025 reviews" stat unless
   you can show where it came from. Replace the whole block with one honest line until the
   preschool results exist:
   > "Now onboarding our first local businesses. Real results posted here as they come in."
2. **Make the buttons work.** All CTAs currently go to `#`.
   - "Start getting reviews" and "Get reviews" go to the signup page, or, if signup is not
     self-serve yet, to a short form (name, business, phone) that you follow up on by hand.
   - "Book a demo" goes to a calendar link (Calendly, Google Calendar appointment page, or
     similar; any of these are free).
   - "Secure", "No Contracts", "Cancel Anytime" become plain text badges, not links.
3. **Replace "pricing" with a founding-customer offer**, since pricing is not decided:
   > "Free for our first 25 local businesses. No card. We just ask for honest feedback."
   This gets customers in, which is what you need right now, and it is true.
4. **Fix the "only shares positive reviews" claim** so it does not describe review gating
   (see gameplan section 9). Suggested rewrite of the "AI Filters & Posts the Best Ones" step:
   > "AI picks your best reviews and turns them into posts for Google, Facebook and Instagram."
   And "Sentiment Detection" under Features:
   > "Spots your best reviews to feature, and flags unhappy ones so you can respond fast."
5. **Add "Log in" to the nav** once the app has a URL.

## Should do in the same pass

6. Footer social icons: remove Google+ (dead since 2019) and Pinterest. Keep Facebook,
   Instagram, and add LinkedIn and YouTube. Each must link to a real profile or be removed.
7. "Power Up Your Blue-Collar Business" becomes something that fits preschools and restaurants
   too, for example "Built for family-owned local businesses competing with the big chains."
8. The "EXPLORE" nav item links to `?p=1053`. Give that page a real address or remove the item.
9. Confirm all five FAQ questions have answers. The pasted copy only showed the first.
   Suggested answers for the four that were blank:
   - *Is any technical setup required?* "No. Connect your Google Business Profile and social
     accounts with a couple of clicks, then add customers by hand or from a spreadsheet."
   - *How often are reviews monitored?* State the real interval once the pilot measures it.
   - *Can I customize the messages?* "Yes, every message is editable before it goes out."
     (Confirm this is true in the product first.)
   - *How often do you ask my customers?* "Once after each visit, with up to N polite
     reminders if they forget. Never more than that, and they can opt out at any time."
     Fill in N from the product.
10. "Developed by Hesper IT Labs" in the footer: optional, but it reads as an agency template.
    Consider removing.
11. Add a short privacy note near the signup form about how customer phone numbers are used.
    The Privacy Policy page should cover SMS consent and opt-out; check that it does.

## After the pilot has numbers

12. Put the preschool before/after on the homepage with screenshots and a date.
13. Replace the AI testimonial slot with the one real parent or owner quote, with permission.
14. Add a Pricing page.
